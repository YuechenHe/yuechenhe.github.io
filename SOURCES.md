## Deliberate limits

- “Ph.D. studies · Chip design” combines the user's stated study status/field with the public NYU entry. “2026 – Present” uses the listed start year and current student status. The profile's future end year was not presented as a completed degree.
- UESTC's degree title and attendance dates were not visible in a reliably attributable entry, so remain unspecified. Other partially hidden education rows were not assigned institutions.
- No job titles, employment dates, adviser, email, GitHub/Scholar account, CV, final grades or full publication citation were recoverable with sufficient confidence.
- No claims of fabrication or measured performance were added for the processor project. It is labeled schematic/layout work.
- Initials are used in place of an unavailable portrait. All gallery image slots are explicit placeholders. There are no fictional photographs, rides, routes or distances.
- A blank publication list means “details to be added,” not a claim that no publications exist.
